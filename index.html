About

I am a hands-on technician. My training covered the full life cycle of an ICT system — assembling a PC, installing and configuring an operating system, setting up LAN and Wi-Fi connectivity, maintaining printers and other peripherals, and troubleshooting faults down to the component level.

I like structured problem solving: reproduce the fault, isolate the cause, fix it, then record what was done so the same issue is faster to resolve next time.
